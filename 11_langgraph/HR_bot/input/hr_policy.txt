1. Classification of Job Responsibility Levels
At LogiCom.in, job roles are classified into four primary bands: Direction, Integration,
Managerial, and Execution. This classification is essential for establishing clear expectations,
responsibilities, and growth opportunities for employees.
1.1 Direction
●
Role: Drive and impact the overall strategy, top-line, and bottom-line of the company.
●
Examples: CEO, CFO, COO
●
Responsibilities: Cascading strategy into functional goals, revenue generation,
business support, ensuring cross-departmental collaboration, and overseeing overall
company performance.
1.2 Integration
●
Role: Head verticals or sub-verticals with proven expertise.
●
Examples: Head of Logistics, Head of IT, Head of Marketing
●
Responsibilities: Deliver results focused on sector/functional goals, manage teams,
ensure alignment with overall strategy, and promote inter-departmental synergy.
1.3 Managerial
●
Role: Lead specific areas within verticals/sub-verticals.
●
Examples: Logistics Manager, IT Manager, Sales Manager
●
Responsibilities: Supervise teams, ensure efficient execution of tasks, meet
departmental objectives, and mentor team members for professional growth.
1.4 Execution
●
Role: Execute tasks under supervision, contribute to common departmental goals.
●
Examples: Logistics Coordinator, IT Support Specialist, Sales Associate
●
Responsibilities: Perform day-to-day operations, assist in project implementation,
maintain operational efficiency, and support continuous improvement initiatives.

●
Applicability: Applies to all employees regardless of gender.
●

6. Information Security Policy
The Information Security Policy at LogiCom.in aims to protect the company’s information assets
from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction, ensuring the confidentiality,
integrity, and availability of information.
6.1 Objective
●
To safeguard information assets and support a secure work environment.
6.2 Applicability
●
Scope: All employees and vendors with access to company information.
●
Enforcement: All users must comply with this policy; violations may lead to disciplinary
actions, including termination, depending on the severity and context of the violation.
6.3 Key Principles
●
Confidentiality: Ensure that sensitive information is accessed only by authorized
personnel.
●
Integrity: Maintain the accuracy and reliability of information and systems.
●
Availability: Ensure information and resources are available to authorized users when
needed.
●
Regulatory Compliance: Adhere to all relevant legal and regulatory requirements.
6.4 Security Measures
●
Access Control: Implement user authentication and authorization to restrict access to
sensitive information.
●
Data Encryption: Use encryption to protect data in transit and at rest.
●
Network Security: Use firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and regular network
monitoring to safeguard network infrastructure.
●
Incident Response: Establish protocols for reporting and responding to security
breaches.
●
Training: Provide regular training to employees on information security best practices
and policies.
6.5 Reporting and Investigation
●
Reporting: All security incidents must be reported to the Information Security team
immediately.
●
Investigation: The Information Security team will investigate all reported incidents and
take appropriate corrective actions, ensuring transparency and support throughout the
process.

8. Leave Policy
LogiCom.in provides various types of leave to support employees' work-life balance and
personal needs.
8.1 Types of Leave
8.1.1 Casual Leave (CL)
●
Eligibility: All regular employees, consultants, and advisors.
●
Entitlement: 12 days per year.
●
Conditions: CL can be taken for half-day; cannot exceed 4 days at a stretch, including
holidays.
8.1.2 Sick Leave (SL)
●
Eligibility: All regular employees, consultants, and advisors.
●
Entitlement: 6 days per year.
●
Conditions: Medical proof required for leave exceeding 2 days; no limit on
accumulation; not encashable.
8.1.3 Privilege Leave (PL)
●
Eligibility: All regular employees.
●
Entitlement: 24 days per year.
●
Conditions: Maximum accumulation up to 180 days; encashable only at separation.
8.1.4 Maternity Leave
●
Eligibility: Female employees with at least 80 days of work in the preceding 12 months.
●
Entitlement: 26 weeks; 12 weeks for adoptive/commissioning mothers.
●
Conditions: Encouraged to report to duty for one day after maternity leave to ensure a
smooth transition back to work, but flexibility and additional support are provided if
needed.
8.1.5 Paternity Leave
●
Eligibility: Male employees.
●
Entitlement: 3 days on two occasions during spouse’s confinement.
8.1.6 Extraordinary Leave
●
Eligibility: Regular employees.
●
Entitlement: Up to 180 days in entire service.
●
Conditions: For extraordinary circumstances like accidents or critical illness.
8.1.7 Leave Without Pay (LOP)
●
Eligibility: Regular employees.
●
Entitlement: Up to 90 days per year.
●
Conditions: Applicable if all other leave types are exhausted.
8.1.8 Compensatory Off (C-Off)
●
Eligibility: Employees in JRL LI to LJ.
●
Entitlement: Compensatory leave for working on weekly offs/national holidays.
●
Conditions: Must be availed within 2 months or it will lapse

11. Performance Management Process (PMP)
11.1 Objective The Performance Management Process (PMP) at LogiCom.in aims to enhance
employee performance, align individual goals with organizational objectives, and foster
professional development through regular feedback and evaluations.
11.2 Components
11.2.1 Goal Setting
●
Annual Goals: Each employee sets specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and
time-bound (SMART) goals at the beginning of the year.
●
Weightage: Goals are assigned weightages based on their importance to the
employee's overall performance.
●
Planned Targets: Each goal is quantified with a clear timeline for completion.
11.2.2 Mid-Year Review
●
Purpose: Review progress, identify challenges, and adjust goals if necessary.
●
Process: A formal discussion between the appraiser and appraisee to provide
constructive feedback and support.
11.2.3 Annual Appraisal
●
Purpose: Evaluate overall performance for the year.
●
Process: The appraiser and appraisee discuss the achievement of goals, and the
appraiser assigns an annual performance rating based on the following scale:
○
OP – Outstanding Performer
○
EP – Exceeding Performer
○
VGP – Very Good Performer
○
CP – Consistent Performer
○
NP – Non Performer
11.2.4 Continuous Feedback
●
Objective: Encourage ongoing communication between managers and employees.
●
Process: Regular check-ins to discuss progress, address concerns, and provide
guidance.
11.3 Development Plans
●
Individual Development Plans (IDPs): Created during appraisals to outline training and
development activities that support career growth.
11.4 Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)
●
Objective: Assist underperforming employees to meet performance standards.
●
Process: Clearly defined goals, timelines, and support mechanisms.

13. Skip Level Meetings
13.1 Objective Skip level meetings provide a platform for open communication between
employees and their manager's manager, fostering transparency and trust.
13.2 Eligibility
●
Scope: All regular employees excluding part-time advisors and consultants.
13.3 Procedure
●
Frequency: Conducted half-yearly or as needed.
●
Format: Group meetings (6-8 employees) or one-on-one sessions.
●
Discussion Topics: Workplace improvements, business performance, clarity on
systems/policies.
●
Follow-up: Skip level manager resolves queries within two weeks.
13.4 Do's and Don'ts
13.4.1 Skip Level Manager
●
Do: Build rapport, discuss broad themes, cover agenda topics, and encourage feedback.
●
Don't: Make commitments without stakeholder checks, limit discussions to constructive
criticism.
13.4.2 Employee
●
Do: Be aware of the agenda, be open and ask questions, provide suggestions and
feedback.
●
Don't: Raise salary, performance reward, or increment-related questions.

Building Fund Fee/Donation: Reimbursement for up to two children’s school admission

18. Talent Management
18.1 Objective The Talent Management policy aims to identify, develop, and retain
high-potential employees, ensuring a robust leadership pipeline and fostering a culture of
continuous improvement.
18.2 Components
18.2.1 Talent Identification
●
Criteria: Based on performance appraisals, potential assessments, and leadership
qualities.
●
Tools: 360-degree feedback, psychometric assessments, and performance reviews.
18.2.2 Development Programs
●
Leadership Development: Programs designed to enhance leadership skills and
prepare employees for higher responsibilities.
●
Career Pathing: Clear career progression paths with defined milestones and required
competencies.
18.2.3 Succession Planning
●
Objective: Ensure continuity in key roles by preparing successors.
●
Process: Identify critical positions, potential successors, and develop Individual
Development Plans (IDPs).
18.2.4 Performance Management
●
Ongoing Feedback: Regular performance discussions between managers and
employees.
●
Annual Appraisals: Comprehensive review of performance and setting of future goals.
18.2.5 Retention Strategies
●
Engagement Programs: Initiatives to enhance employee engagement and job
satisfaction.
●
Recognition and Rewards: Regular recognition of achievements and contributions.
19. Talent Acquisition
19.1 Objective To attract and recruit the best talent for LogiCom.in, ensuring a seamless and
efficient hiring process aligned with the company’s strategic goals.
19.2 Recruitment Process
19.2.1 Manpower Planning
●
Annual Operating Plan (AOP): HODs prepare manpower plans as part of the AOP,
detailing required positions and budget.
●
Approval: Staffing approvals by CEO/GCXO for GM and above; business heads for
other levels.
19.2.2 Sourcing Candidates
●
Internal Job Posting (IJP): Preference for internal candidates to fill vacancies.
●
Employee Referrals: Encouraging employees to refer suitable candidates.
●
External Sources: Job portals, recruitment consultants, campus recruitments, and
advertisements.
19.2.3 Screening and Shortlisting
●
Initial Screening: Conducted by BHR/CHR to assess education, experience, role fit,
and cultural fit.
●
Shortlisting: Selected candidates’ profiles sent to relevant business/functional heads for
evaluation.
19.2.4 Assessment and Selection
●
Technical/Aptitude Tests: For relevant roles to evaluate candidates' skills.
●
Psychometric Tests: For LD/PD and above, to assess competencies and potential.
●
Interviews: Conducted by a panel including HR and functional managers.
19.2.5 Pre-Employment Medical Check-Up
●
Requirement: Mandatory medical check-up for all selected candidates before joining.
●
Process: Conducted through a network of approved hospitals.
